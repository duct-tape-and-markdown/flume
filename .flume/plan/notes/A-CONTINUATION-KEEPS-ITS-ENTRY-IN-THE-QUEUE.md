# The put-down kinds now differ on the verdict, but not on the record

Two things the next tick on this family should read.

**A precedence nobody decided.** A commit writing *both* notes under its tag
is possible (the fence admits both) and the spec says nothing about it. The
predicate in `harness/chain.ts` reads the park first, so such a commit is a
park: only one can be true of an entry, and the reading that keeps the work
in front of plan is the safer one. It is a judgment call, not a cite — if
that is wrong, the fix is one line there and a case beside the two new ones.

**The engine's record still cannot tell the two apart.** The `shipped`
predicate answers a boolean, so a continuation and a park both land as a
`not-shipped` prior attempt. `standingRefusals` (`harness/standingRefusal.ts`)
classifies `not-shipped` as plan's to reconcile, so as of this commit a
continuation wakes the inbox slice and renders in `<build-records>` as a park
whose note plan cannot find — the drain does not list the continuing
directory. That is exactly the wall
A-CONTINUATION-ROUTES-BACK-TO-BUILD-NOT-THE-DRAIN names, and it is not
closable inside the chain: the engine reports one mode for both, so the
routing either needs the chain's put-down kind on a reporting surface
(`.claude/rules/engineering.md`, *A fact the engine holds is reported, never
rediscovered*) or a second fact beside `mode`. Worth sizing that entry with
the engine leg included rather than as a `standingRefusal.ts` table edit — as
written its `files` may be short.
