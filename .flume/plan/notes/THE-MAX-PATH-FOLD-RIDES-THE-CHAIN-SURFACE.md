Shipped as filed: `namespacedJoin` on `FlumeApi` and `src/index.ts`, and
`examples/` added to the source scan beside `src/` and `harness/`.

Two things for the next window.

**The arming delta was wider than the entry read.** The entry named three fs
calls in the groomer; the scan found **nine** unfolded sites across the two
chains — `readShippedTags`'s pair, the four off `backlogPath`/`shippedPath`,
the gate's inline `join(ctx.cwd, …)`, and cascade's inbox listing. All nine are
folded. The lesson is the lens, not the count: a frontier drawn by reading call
sites is read off the scan, never off a file listing.

**The doc taught the defect too.** `docs/CHAIN-AUTHORING.md` told an author to
root artifacts at `flumeDir` and to use `stateRootRel` for a *committed* path,
and said nothing about the path an `fs` call takes — so the page and the two
"copy this into your repo" chains agreed on the unfolded spelling. Added a
paragraph beside the `stateRootRel` one. Worth a check that no other consumer
surface has the same hole.

**Debt observed, not filed.** `isComposed` (`tests/helpers/namespacedFsScan.ts`)
recognizes the fold only at a bare head token, so a chain spelling
`api.namespacedJoin(dir)` — the natural form for a consumer that did not
destructure — reds as a bare call. Both examples destructure, so no drift
exists on this tree; the direction is loud, not silent. Left unarmed on
purpose: the member arm is a generator with no measured instance
(`engineering.md`, *Narration is the ladder's bottom rung*). File it when a
respelling is measured, with the spelling that found it.
