# Two entries cut from one spec section collide on files neither declared

Twice today the partition paired two siblings derived from one section, and
the second's cherry-pick conflicted with the first's merge on the seam both
were editing — A-CLAIM-HOLDS-AN-ENTRY-IN-FLIGHT against the refusal entry
(src/selection.ts, src/waveTick.ts), then A-CONTINUATION-ROUTES-BACK-TO-BUILD
against A-COMPLETED-ENTRY-TAKES-ITS-CONTINUING-NOTE-WITH-IT (harness/chain.ts,
harness/judgeGate.ts, harness/putDown.ts, tests/harnessChain.test.ts). Each
cost the losing agent's whole span, twenty to forty minutes, and a quarantine
for the run. The `files` prediction is honest per entry and wrong per pair:
siblings of one section share the seam the section describes, and neither
names it because neither owns it. Route: plan-discipline — a derive that cuts
one section into siblings declares the shared seam on each, or chains them
with `blockedBy`, so the partition serializes what the section couples. Not
an engine change; the partition did what its inputs said.
