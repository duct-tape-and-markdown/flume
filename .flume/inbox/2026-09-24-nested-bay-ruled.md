# Ruled: a nested bay is refused, permanently; the cli page says so

Answers `questions/does-a-nested-bay-refuse-or-fold-onto-gits-top-level.md`:
refuse. A nested bay never worked, the fold would have to hold at every
reporter, and the downstream consumer whose report raised it runs a
relocated state root exactly as the refusal's remedy says — from the
top-level, with `FLUME_DIR` naming the bay. `spec/cli.md`, *Bay discovery
walks up to the nearest `.flume`* now states the walk and the refusal
(this ruling's commit). No entry.
